What it plays

Anything that can be a wallpaper.

The way Wallpaper Engine does it on Windows, reading Wallpaper Engine's own Workshop items to do it. Each monitor gets its own wallpaper, fit, look and playlist, crossfaded, and follows the monitor across ports.

Scenes, natively

Its own renderer, not a Windows program.

Workshop scenes are drawn by apolline-scene, a clean-room renderer on OpenGL or GLES, in the same GL context as pictures, videos and shaders. So a scene gets the same crossfades, looks, pause and frame limits as everything else.

A scene that needs something not done yet is refused up front with the reason, never drawn with parts left out, and goes to linux-wallpaperengine if you have it.

One scene, many monitors

It runs once, however many screens show it.

A moving wallpaper shown on several monitors runs once, into a canvas of its own shape, and each monitor shows its part. Particles, scripts and effects cost the same for one screen or three, and one decode feeds every monitor showing the same video.

Free when unseen

Covered, locked or asleep, it stops.

A frame is drawn only when the compositor asks for one, so a wallpaper behind a fullscreen window costs nothing. The wallpaper at the top of this page does the same: scroll away and it pauses.

  • pauses on screen lock and before sleep
  • on battery: keep going, cap at 15 fps and half resolution, or pause
  • under Apolline, pauses for games and performance modes
  • a pause you made stays: unlocking never resumes it

Where it runs

Wayland, X11, and Plasma's own desktop.

A layer-shell surface per monitor, drawn with GLES at each monitor's physical resolution. Plasma gets a wallpaper plugin under its icons and widgets, GNOME a Shell extension, X11 a desktop window per monitor. Here is honestly where it has been run.

Run and checked

  • niri, on real hardware
  • sway
  • labwc
  • X11 with openbox
  • X11 with i3

Layer-shell, should just work

  • Hyprland
  • river
  • Wayfire
  • COSMIC

Written, not run yet

  • KDE Plasma 6, through PipeWire
  • GNOME 45–49
  • AMD and Intel GPUs on real scenes

On one of these? Tell how it went, the app writes a report in one click.

The Wallpapers app

Browse, pick, tune, on any desktop.

apolline-wallpapers is a window in Apolline's look. It talks to the daemon over the same socket as the CLI and starts it if it isn't running.
